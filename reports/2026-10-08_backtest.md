## Evaluator verdict

**Overall: no model ships as-is. negbin is the best candidate once it gets a calibration step. qgbm is the best calibrated model available today.**

- **Not too good to be true.** The best model improves on the league-average baseline by only 6.2% in mean pinball loss and 6.4% in MAE (MAE 4.35 vs 4.64 shots). That is in line with team-form features whose correlation with total shots is about 0.3. Nothing points to leakage.
- **negbin** has the lowest pinball loss overall. It is also the best model in all 7 seasons (tied with qgbm in 2021/22). Its row-paired advantage over qgbm is 0.0147 pinball, with a bootstrap 95% CI of [0.012, 0.018]. **However, it over-covers:** 73.3/82.9/91.9% coverage against targets of 70/80/90, and 85.3% at q=0.8 in Segunda Division. The bounds are about 0.5 shot too low (sharpness at q=0.8: 19.2 vs 19.8 for qgbm). The error is on the safe side, but a conformal/quantile shift fitted on the latest training season should give both calibration and an even lower pinball loss. **Fix this before shipping.**
- **qgbm** is calibrated overall (69.5/79.8/89.8%) and is the sharpest model. Its weak spots are under-coverage at q=0.8 in Greece (76.8%) and Scotland Championship (76.3%), and in 2022/23 (77.3%). It also has a cold-start bias of -0.9 shots. It beats team_avg on pinball by only 0.010 (CI [0.007, 0.012]).
- **team_avg** is a strong, well-calibrated baseline (worst season 1.4pp off target). Any shipped model must beat it per competition, not just overall.
- **league_avg** is badly calibrated per season (up to 9pp), as expected from a static distribution. Keep it as the reference baseline only.
- **Partial 2026/27 (n=1,385):** every model under-predicts (bias of -0.7 to -1.7 shots). Shot volume is up early this season. Coverage still holds for the team-based models (team_avg 80.9%, negbin 81.7%, qgbm 80.4% at q=0.8). Watch this as the season progresses; a monthly refit (`--refit month`) would adapt faster.
- **Next steps for the modeler:** (1) add a post-hoc conformal shift to negbin, per competition or tier, then re-run this backtest; (2) consider blending negbin and qgbm; (3) re-run with `--refit month` before any ship decision, since that matches production.
