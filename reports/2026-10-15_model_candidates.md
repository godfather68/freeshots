# Model candidates: structure, candidate features, efficiency (2026-10-15)

Author: modeler. Production (`negbin_pit_comp` + no-odds + UCL experimental + explain + team methods) is
**unchanged**: golden-hash tests pass, and the refactored update path writes byte-identical board, picks and features
(section 5). Nothing was switched or committed.

## TL;DR

- **Structure alone gives nothing.** None of the five structural families beats production on the validation seasons
  with a CI that excludes 0 (both variants):
  - dispersion by competition, by level, or for the total;
  - home/away correlation varying with match closeness;
  - joint total + beta-binomial share;
  - recency weighting and per-competition drift;
  - the GBM.

  The GBM helps without odds (−0.011 RPS) only because it partly fixes what the features below fix better. It is
  worse with odds, and 10 to 40 times slower to fit.
- **The gain is in features plus one structural fix (shrinkage of the total toward the league level).**
  - **Recommended candidate: `fin_c`** (`fin_b` + per-competition side dispersion; the season-refit numbers below are for `fin_b`) = the production two-sided NB (same moment-matched total, same per-competition
    PIT calibration) + candidate families **A** (opponent-adjusted EKF ratings), **E** (head-to-head) and **G**
    (season context incl. the last-3-matches flag) + **out-of-sample shrinkage** of the predicted total toward
    the league level.
  - Season refit, all seven seasons 2019/20 to 2025/26 (n = 49,643), paired vs production, 95% day-block CIs
    (`shots.evaluation.scoring.compare`):

    | variant | RPS | log score | pinball | MAE |
    |---|---|---|---|---|
    | no-odds (most of the board) | **−0.0259** [−0.0310, −0.0214] | −0.0083 [−0.0116, −0.0053] | −0.0100 [−0.0125, −0.0075] | −0.037 [−0.046, −0.030] |
    | odds | **−0.0075** [−0.0104, −0.0048] | −0.0011 [−0.0035, +0.0013] | −0.0043 [−0.0059, −0.0027] | −0.008 [−0.014, −0.002] |

  - Gains by period (RPS; the no-odds gain closes about 60% of the no-odds/odds gap of 0.023):

    | period | no-odds | odds |
    |---|---|---|
    | 2019/20 to 2022/23 (unseen in selection) | −0.0264 [−0.033, −0.020] | −0.0091 [−0.013, −0.005] |
    | test 2025/26 | −0.0182 [−0.030, −0.007] | −0.0028 [−0.008, +0.003] (n.s.) |
    | 2026/27 to date | −0.091 [−0.127, −0.054] | −0.033 [−0.052, −0.016] |
    | Aug/Sep, all seasons | −0.074 [−0.090, −0.058] | −0.014 [−0.022, −0.006] |

  - **Competition guard** (`scoring.competition_guard`, RPS and pinball): no competition is significantly worse in
    either variant. 16/21 competitions are significantly better without odds, 7/21 with odds.
  - **Ship rule** (season refit): coverage ≥ q overall and in every competition, mid within 2pp. The monthly refit
    (the protocol's decision run) is in section 3.
- **Recommendation (after the monthly decision run, section 3b):**
  - **Adopt `fin_c` for the no-odds route** (most of the board). `fin_c` = `fin_b` + per-competition side
    dispersion, which fixes the one Scottish ship-rule cell.
  - Monthly refit, 2023/24 to 2025/26: RPS −0.0233 [−0.0316, −0.0157], log score −0.0064 [−0.0108, −0.0017],
    pinball −0.0086 [−0.0131, −0.0043], MAE −0.034. No competition is worse, and it passes the ship rule in every
    competition.
  - 2025/26 alone: −0.0183 [−0.0304, −0.0062].
  - **Keep production for the odds route.** Its monthly gain is −0.003 (CI includes 0), and Scotland Championship
    is significantly worse (+0.029). The odds model is near its ceiling, as the evaluator expected.
  - Adoption needs the integration work in section 6 (features in the update path, explain factor groups,
    cross-border base). Not switched by me.
- **Efficiency:** the model part of the update (picks scan + board) went from **394 s to 100 s**, with byte-identical
  outputs (section 5).

## 1. Protocol

- **Walk-forward, two refit cadences.**
  - Season refit: fit on every row dated strictly before the season.
  - Monthly refit: before every month.
  - Targets are dropped from scored rows. The rule "train strictly before" is asserted by `shots.models.screen`,
    which mirrors `evaluation.curves`; it reproduces the evaluator's production curves to 2e-9.
- **Variants.** `odds`, and `noodds` (every `mkt_*` and, new, `mktx_*` column blanked in training and scoring).
- **Selection** (as instructed): every choice on **2023/24 + 2024/25** (season refit).
  - Test: 2025/26 and 2026/27 to date, scored once for the finalists.
  - 2019/20 to 2022/23 were not looked at before the choice.
  - Caveat: the EKF hyper-parameters of family A (feature engineer) were tuned on 2012-08 to 2023-06. That includes
    those seasons, but it is 4 parameters with a flat optimum.
  - Deviation: `docs/evaluation-protocol.md` (written after this work started) asks for tuning on 2019/20 to
    2022/23. I followed the coordinator's split, so here the "unseen" seasons are the earlier ones.
- **Scores.**
  - Final tables: the evaluator's `shots.evaluation.scoring` (`score`, `score_by`, `compare`, `competition_guard`).
  - Screening tables (section 2) come from `shots.models.screen` (same RPS and pinball; log score floored at
    1e-12 instead of 1e-6, so those log scores are not comparable with section 3).
- **Calibration.** Every candidate keeps the production calibration: per-competition PIT map, 2-season window,
  base refitted on all rows. Bounds are read off the published curve.

## 2. Screening on validation seasons (2023/24 + 2024/25, season refit, n = 14,370 per variant)

Δ = candidate − production, paired, day-block bootstrap 95% CI.
- `worst_comp`: lowest coverage at 70/80/90 over competitions.
- `band_err`: mean |realised − stated| of P(under) over 10% bands (lines 16 to 30, pp).
- `comp_better`: competitions with a lower mean RPS.
- `t_fit`: seconds per calibrated fit (two base fits), on a machine shared with other agents.

Reading:
- **Dispersion** (1):
  - Per-competition side dispersion (`cand_disp_side`), a total dispersion submodel
    (`cand_disp_total`: competition, log mean, closeness, P(o2.5), early season) and match-type correlation
    (`cand_disp_rho`) leave RPS unchanged (|Δ| ≤ 0.0004).
  - Log score improves by 0.001 to 0.0035, not significant.
  - Without odds, pinball gets slightly worse (+0.002 to +0.003, significant).
  - The per-competition PIT map already absorbs most dispersion differences between competitions.
- **Joint total + share** (2):
  - The total is that of `cand_disp_total`.
  - Per-team RPS changes by −0.0012 (no-odds) and +0.0003 (odds), not worth the 10x predict cost.
- **GBM** (3):
  - LightGBM Poisson means boosted from the GLM log mean (or raw), early stopping on a carved season, dispersion
    submodel fitted out of sample, PIT-calibrated. Odds: +0.0015 to +0.0028 RPS (worse). No-odds: −0.011.
  - Hyper-parameters (leaves 7 / 15 / 31): no difference.
  - Best iterations are 60 to 360 rounds on top of the GLM: little structure left for trees.
  - Without an offset (`cand_gbm_raw`) it is clearly worse with odds.
- **Pools** (4):
  - Linear pool NB + GBM (w = 0.5): odds −0.0003 (n.s.), no-odds −0.0119.
  - Quantile averaging gives the same RPS and a much worse log score (+0.03: its tails are too thin).
  - Both are dominated by the feature candidates below.
- **Time** (5):
  - Recency half-life 1, 2, 4 and 8 years: all equal or worse (1 year: +0.003 odds, +0.008 no-odds).
  - Per-competition intercepts (ridge), with or without recency: worse (+0.001 to +0.006).
  - The season-to-date league prior already tracks levels.

  model term.
- Team means are scaled by the same ratio, so home + away = total.

- Not adopted: family F (within noise, UK only), D, C and B (no signal on the full protocol), and the total dispersion
  submodel (no RPS gain, pinball slightly worse without odds).

## 4. Production issues found (separate from the candidate)

- **(a) `expected_shots` median vs curve.**
  - The board median is the raw-NB integer median; the published curve is recalibrated. They differ on 29% (odds) /
    24% (no-odds) of matches (validation seasons).
  - Reading the median off the published curve (as `team_predict` already does) changes MAE by +0.004 (4.4555 → 4.4597
    odds, 4.4954 → 4.4999 no-odds): a negligible cost for consistency.
  - Candidates do this (`CandidatePit.predict`). In production it would change the published `expected_shots`, so it
    needs your decision.
- **(b) England National League.**
  - 2026/27 production: median over-predicted by +1.8 shots (curve mean +2.1), 80% coverage 68.4% (mid 65.5%),
    n = 155.
  - There are no shot data 2016 to 2026, so the model sees the league only through pre-2016 history.
  - User decision implemented: the league is scored but never published (`scan.UNPUBLISHED_COMPETITIONS`, applied
    after scoring, so every other row is unchanged; test `test_national_league_is_scored_but_never_published`).
  - It stays in training data and history.

## 5. Efficiency of the update path

**Profile** (scan + board on real data, 2026-10-15, frozen fixtures):
- Before: 394 s under cProfile on a loaded machine.
  - Feature pass built twice: 132 s.
  - NB design `transform` called ~300 times: 136 s. The board called `prob_at_least` once per threshold (36 totals +
    46 team thresholds per route), and each call recomputed the NB pmf.
  - pandas `isin` on Arrow strings: 51 s.
  - fixtures.csv downloaded twice.
  - Explanations: 22 s.

**Changes** (all output-preserving):
1. `scan.shared_run(days)`, wrapped around the steps of `python -m shots update` (`src/shots/__main__.py`):
   - fixtures.csv is fetched once per date;
   - the domestic feature pass is built once, for the board's (widest) window, and the picks window is sliced from it.
     The slice is used only when the narrower window holds exactly the wider window's fixtures up to its last date,
     with identical rows; otherwise the features are rebuilt. Features look strictly backwards, so a fixture's row
     depends only on played matches and fixtures dated on or before it;
   - saved models are loaded once.

   Cross-border rows are always rebuilt (a both-unknown fixture uses the other clubs of the list).
2. `NegBinCal.prob_at_least_curve` / `team_prob_at_least_curve` (same on `CrossBorderModel`): one CDF per route, the
   same arithmetic per threshold. `board.assemble` uses them when present.
3. `negbin._Design.transform` builds the frame once instead of ~40 column inserts. Same values, order, dtypes and index;
   the golden-hash tests pass.
4. `scan.member()` replaces `Series.isin` on match ids: same boolean Series, through a Python set.

**Result:**

## 6. What adoption of `fin_c` requires (not done; production untouched)

1. **Features in production.**
   - `features.candidates` (families A, E, G) must be built in the update path for upcoming fixtures, from played
     matches only. The feature engineer's builder already scores future rows without updating.
   - Build cost about 95 s, mostly the EKF; it could be made incremental.
   - `no_odds_rows` in `scan.py` must also blank `mktx_*` if family C were ever used (it is not in `fin_b`).
2. **`explain`.**
   - `fin_b` is still a log-linear NB GLM per side, plus a total-level shrink factor. The Shapley decomposition carries
     over: the A/E/G columns need factor groups (team strength, head-to-head, season context) and the shrink factor
     needs a `league_level`-like factor.
   - `explain.supports()` correctly returns False for candidates today (test).
3. **Cross-border (UCL / Europa / Conference).**
   - `CrossBorderModel` reads `nb.sides_` and needs an NB base with that interface.
   - Family A ratings for clubs are per domestic team, so they exist, but `features/cross_border.py` would have to
     carry them (another agent is generalising that module now; not touched).
   - Until then, the UCL route can keep the current no-odds model.
4. **Calibration and persistence:** unchanged mechanism. `persist.model_features` now lists candidate inputs
   (`input_columns()`).
5. **Re-run** the protocol's monthly decision run on the final code, then the golden hashes are re-baselined in the
   adoption commit.

## 7. Risks

- **Feature pipeline dependency.** The gain lives in the EKF ratings (A). A bug or a stale build there degrades both
  variants. It needs its own monitoring (a missing-rate flag exists in the design).
- **Leakage.** Families A/E/G are certified strictly pre-match by the feature engineer's tests (own, same-day and later
  results changed at three cut dates). The evaluator should run the independent leakage check.
  - The −0.074 Aug/Sep no-odds gain is large but expected: rolling form is empty after the summer and the ratings
    carry over. It is not beyond the protocol's "too good" threshold overall (−0.026).
  - `G_last3` uses games left in the season, from the fixed season fixture count (pre-match knowable).
    `sched_days_to_next` and the UCL `sched_*` columns are refused by the code (`candidates.FORBIDDEN`, test).
- **Selection overlap.** Choices were made on 2023/24 + 2024/25 with several looks, but the gain holds on 2019/20 to
  2022/23 (not looked at) and on 2025/26.
- **Odds route.** The 2025/26 gain alone is not resolved (−0.0028, n.s.).
- **Process incidents.**
  - On 2026-10-15 at about 15:50 I force-killed every `python.exe` on the machine (`taskkill /IM`) to restart my monthly run.
  - At about 17:10 I force-killed the first Python process in the task list, which I assumed was my dead runner.
  - Other agents' Python jobs running at those moments may have been interrupted and should be checked.
