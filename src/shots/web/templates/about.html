How it works

The short version, then the detail.

What the percentage means

P is the model's estimate of the chance that a match has at least N total shots: both teams together, on and off target, blocked shots included. At 24+ and 78%, roughly 78 of 100 comparable matches should reach 24 shots.

"80% sure: at least 27" is the same idea read the other way round: the highest whole number the model is 80% confident the match will reach.

Where they come from

A statistical model (negative binomial on each team's recent shot rates, home and away, rest days and league level), then recalibrated league by league on recent seasons so the percentages mean what they say. No judgement or guessing goes into a number; no model runs when you open this page. It only shows the board that was last built.

Team shots experimental

Each row also shows the expected shots for each team ("17 – 13", home first), and you can filter on one team reaching a count. These team numbers are experimental: the team bounds pass the overall calibration checks on 2025/26 but not yet the per-competition check, so we make no per-league confidence claim for them and they deserve more caution than the match totals.

Odds and no odds

Bookmaker odds sharpen the picture, but most fixtures this far ahead have none published. Those are scored by a separate model trained without odds, and badged no odds. Using the odds model with odds missing would overstate its confidence, so that never happens.

Calibration

A model is calibrated when its "70%" calls come true about 70% of the time. The track record checks this on matches the model had not seen. Bounds are whole numbers, so reality tends to land slightly on the safe side of the stated figure.

Be careful with small samples

  • cold start means a team has fewer than 5 earlier matches in our data (promoted sides, new competitions). Its numbers are less reliable.
  • A handful of matches proves nothing in either direction. Judge the model over hundreds of matches, not a weekend.
  • Lineups, injuries and red cards are not in the model. These are pre-match probabilities, not certainties.
